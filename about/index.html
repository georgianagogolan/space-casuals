The studio

Our
Story.

Space Casuals is a Bucharest-based architecture and interior design studio operating across three disciplines: architecture, interior design and retail design. Founded in 2003 under the name Zebra 3 by Alexandru Călin, the studio adopted the Space Casuals identity in 2013.

We are a dynamic group of architects and designers — young, highly motivated, and experienced both locally and internationally. Our approach is what we call hiper-pragmatică: creative, prompt, and always grounded in real outcomes for real people.
