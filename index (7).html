Office Design · Bacău · 2014

Softescu
2.0.

Softescu 2.0 extends The Craftsman Workshop through a more defined industrial language and a family of custom black-metal furniture.

Inspired by workshop benches, the pieces were designed by Space Casuals and produced by Sudometal. Exposed concrete, graffiti and North American garage-comic references continue the creative workplace identity.
