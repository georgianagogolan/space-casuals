Hospitality · Sinaia · 2013

Restaurant
Kuib.

Set beside the George Enescu memorial house, Restaurant Kuib extends into nature through a laminated timber structure and panoramic glazing that opens on three sides.

A massive oak bar grows from the floor, handmade Banat furniture gives the dining room character, and a working century-old piano connects the restaurant to the cultural history of its setting.
