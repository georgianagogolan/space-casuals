Interior Design · Bucharest · 2018

Studio
Alb.

Studio Alb transforms a 1960s apartment near Cișmigiu Park into a calm, light-filled 40 sqm home.

The balcony becomes part of the living space, while white surfaces, oak, black accents and an interwar-style chequered tile floor create a Nordic-leaning interior rooted in Bucharest. The furniture was designed specifically for the apartment.
