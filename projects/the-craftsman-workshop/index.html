Interior Design · Bacău · 2014

The Craftsman
Workshop.

The Craftsman Workshop is a creative atelier between a North American underground garage and a hands-on maker space.

Raw finishes, custom furniture, recycled-pallet sofas and a foosball table lend energy to the workplace. A meeting room clad in unfinished timber becomes a quiet counterpoint to the open studio.
