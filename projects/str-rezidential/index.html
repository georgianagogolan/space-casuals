Architecture · Bucharest

STR
Rezidential.

STR Rezidential brings two building volumes together above shared underground parking, oriented towards Lake Străulești.

Triangular bay windows frame the water for each residence, while white geometric volumes and timber-and-metal terrace screens calibrate privacy. Colourful shared spaces provide a lively counterpoint to the disciplined exterior.
