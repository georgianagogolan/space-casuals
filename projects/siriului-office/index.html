Architecture · Bucharest · 2013

Siriului
Office.

Siriului Office is a business support and development centre for small and medium enterprises in north Bucharest, financed with European funds.

Its modular plan lets each floor operate as autonomous rental units while sharing meeting rooms, conference halls and essential services. Vertical windows, horizontal brise-soleil, corrugated metal cladding and three Trespa-panel boxes give the facade a measured commercial identity.
