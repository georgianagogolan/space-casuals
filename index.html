Collaborators

Brands
& Partners.

Every Space Casuals project is the result of a wider network of trusted craftspeople, manufacturers and consultants. These are the partners whose quality of work we stand behind, and who help us bring our designs from drawing to reality.

Pentagram
Retail Design Partner
Strategic retail design partnership — Space Casuals principal Alexandru Călin and architect Georgiana Gogolan collaborate with Pentagram on commercial and brand-environment projects.
Sudometal
Custom Metal Furniture
Fabricator of the bespoke blackened-steel furniture collection designed by Space Casuals for Softescu 2.0. Precision metalwork for interior applications.
Mobilaunicat
Custom Furniture Production
Manufacturer of Space Casuals' custom furniture designs including the full suite of bespoke joinery and cabinetry for Studio Alb.
Domicilio
Lighting & Small Furniture
Lighting supplier and small furniture partner for multiple Space Casuals projects. Curated selection aligned with the studio's material palette.
KARE
Accessories & Furniture
Accessories and accent furniture supplier for The Craftsman Workshop, contributing to the space's layered industrial-craft aesthetic.
Wall & Deco
Wallcoverings
Decorative wallpaper and surface treatments used across hospitality and office projects to add texture and graphic depth to interior schemes.
forFITOUT
Office Seating
Ergonomic office chair supplier for the Softescu 2.0 project — balancing workplace performance with the space's design identity.
AMB Design Project
MEP Engineering
Mechanical, electrical and plumbing engineering consultants on architectural projects including Siriului Office and STR Rezidential.
POPP & Asociatii
Structural Engineering
Structural engineering partner on the Siriului Office commercial building — 5,070 sqm of modular, divisible SME workspace in north Bucharest.
